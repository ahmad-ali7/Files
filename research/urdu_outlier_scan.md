# Urdu outlier scan: career, money, skills and AI

Scan run on 21 Oct 2026 for the Ahmad Nawaz Ali channel. Raw data is in `urdu_outliers.csv`.

## Method
- I ran 32 YouTube searches in Urdu, Roman Urdu and English, covering salary, tax, mutual funds, first job, interviews, communication, habits, ChatGPT, Claude, vibe coding and similar topics. They returned 1,252 results from 648 channels.
- I kept the 392 channels that had either a result above 30K views or 2 or more results, and downloaded up to 150 recent long videos for each (Shorts excluded).
- **Outlier** means a video with at least 3× the channel's median views and at least 30K views, uploaded in the last 24 months. That gave 3,609 outliers. After removing off-topic ones (news, cricket, dramas, recipes and so on), 1,751 remain.
- Market tags (PK, IN or "?") are a guess from each channel's titles: Urdu or Devanagari script, and words like Pakistan, FBR, PSX, Nifty or UPSC.

**Limits:**
- Search results favor relevance, so the scan is broad but not exhaustive.
- Upload dates are approximate.
- Ratios are inflated for channels with very low medians.
- View counts are as fetched on 21 Oct 2026.

## Key findings
1. **Topic demand matters more than channel size.** Small Urdu channels broke out on the right topic:
   - Adnan Hussain 2.0 (8K subscribers, 1.8K median) got **327K** on "6 Side Businesses You Can Start With a Job".
   - Pak Tax Lawyer (12K subscribers) got **310K** on a salaried tax-return guide.
   - Money Minded Rehan (18K subscribers) got **286K** on mutual funds.
2. **Corporate career content is the emptiest gap in Urdu.** The top corporate-life outliers are Indian/Hindi, from Freshers GPT (118K subscribers): 942K, 652K and 559K views. I found no Pakistani equivalent.
3. **Money education wins when it's practical and step by step**, as in "how to open an account", "mutual funds explained" and "file your tax return". This fits the education-only rule.
4. **Seasonal demand exists.** Tax-return videos peak from July to September, and the federal budget comes in June. Prepare these in advance.
5. **Audiences are angry about scams.** "Ducky Bhai … fake Shopify course scam" got 3.2M views. Being the honest voice is a positioning advantage. Avoid "earn online with no investment", typing or captcha jobs, and YouTube automation hype.
6. **Faceless "psychology / life lessons / quotes" channels are huge in Urdu** (hundreds of thousands of views each). This isn't our format, but the "psychology of X" framing is worth borrowing.
