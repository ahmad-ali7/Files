## C. Rights: read before using any clip
- **The risk:** a clip from someone else's video can bring a **Content ID claim** (their ads on our video, or the video blocked) or a **copyright strike**. Three strikes end the channel. A short clip doesn't make it safe.
- **Safest, in order:**
  1. **Get written permission** (a WhatsApp, email or YouTube comment reply is fine). Ask for 10 seconds, with credit on screen and in the description.
  2. **Have a local person record a fresh voice note for us** (OPEN_QUESTIONS B2). It's fully ours, and best for the channel.
  3. **Hazara University's 2006–07 cassettes**, with the department's permission.
- **For PTV songs:** don't use them without a licence. Instead, **you can read 1–2 lines of the verse in your own voice**. The 1863 poem itself is long out of copyright; only a particular recording or edition can be protected.
- **What to send me:** the video link and the exact start and end time of the 8–10 seconds. Also tell me what the person says, the speaker's name if given, and whether permission was received.
