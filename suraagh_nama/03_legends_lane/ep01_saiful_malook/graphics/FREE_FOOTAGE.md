# Free footage and photos: Naran and Lake Saiful Malook (checked 24 Nov 2026)

## Bottom line
- **Free video is scarce.** Across the stock sites there are only **5 clips** of the lake or Naran: 2 on Pexels and 3 on Pixabay.
- **YouTube clips licensed CC-BY** (Creative Commons Attribution) are the biggest video pool, but **each one must be checked by hand**.
- **Free photos are plentiful:** about 50 high-resolution lake photos and about 40 Naran photos with open licences.
- **No free night footage of the lake exists.** Night shots must come from our own 3D renders, or from day footage graded to night.

## Licence rules (read once)
| Source | Licence | Commercial (YouTube ads) OK? | Credit needed? | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pexels** | Pexels License | ✅ | No (but credit anyway) | Don't imply the creator endorses us |
| **Pixabay** | Pixabay Content License | ✅ | No (credit anyway) | No recognisable people in misleading use |
| **Wikimedia Commons / Flickr: CC0 or public domain** | No conditions | ✅ | No | — |
| **CC BY 2.0 / 4.0** | Attribution | ✅ | **Yes**: name, licence and link in the description | — |
| **CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0** (most Commons photos) | Attribution, ShareAlike | ✅ | **Yes** | ShareAlike: if we *modify* the photo (heavy grade, composite), the modified photo should carry BY-SA. **Safest: use it unaltered or with a simple pan or zoom, and credit it** |
| **NC or ND licences** (non-commercial, no derivatives) | — | ❌ | — | **Don't use.** A monetised channel counts as commercial |
| **YouTube "CC-BY" videos** | CC BY 3.0 | ✅ | **Yes**: channel name and link | Re-uploaders sometimes mark other people's footage as CC. Use only footage the channel clearly filmed itself |
| **Google Earth Studio** | Google's terms | ✅ (YouTube allowed) | **Yes**: "Google Earth" on screen | Already in our workflow |

**Our own rule:** no identifiable faces. Skip or crop photos and clips with recognisable people. For example, I left out a CC0 photo of a celebrity on Commons.

|---|---|---|
| **Google Earth Studio** (your ES1–ES4 shots) | Aerial moves over the real terrain | Credit "Google Earth" on screen |
