**Rule for this episode:** a new question or twist at least every 45–60 seconds. Each section ends on a question that the next section answers.

**Defaults I used for the open questions** (tell me if you want any of them changed):

**Fact rules:**

- "کہا جاتا ہے" marks tradition.

**Length:** about 865 Urdu words. Your K2 voice-over ran at about 205 Urdu words a minute; a calm human read is usually 170–190. That gives a **4:30–5:00** read with pauses. The `…` marks are pauses. If it runs long, cut the 1883–84 card and the line "1669 میں آراکان…" first.
