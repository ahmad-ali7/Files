# Episode 1, Saiful Malook: gaps, conflicts and what to find

What I've read in full:
- The UNESCO/Hazara University survey: 80 pages of text; pages 60–77 are maps and photos.
- The 1907 Hazara Gazetteer, chapter VIII on the Kagan valley (pp. 202–218) plus the lakes and legend section.
- The 1883–84 gazetteer's lake and route sections, searched in full for the legend.
- *Acta Orientalia* 2014, on fairy lore.
- James Abbott's 1854 *Legends, Ballads, &c.*, searched in full.

All claims are in `DATASET.csv`.

## A. Conflicts in the data (how the script handles them)
| Item | Conflict | Script handling |
|---|---|---|
| Lake height | 3,224 m (Wikipedia) vs 3,500 m (UNESCO 2007) vs 10,718 ft ≈ 3,267 m (1883 and 1907) | Say "about 3,200 metres" |
| Lake depth | 34 m comes only from secondary sources; no survey found | Say "measurements reported about 34 m"; the "bottomless" belief is wrong |
| The prince's homeland | Egypt (Nights, Bakhsh) / Delhi (1907) / "Mughal" (2007) | Use it as a story beat: the prince changed homeland on the way |
| Fairy's name | Badi'at al-Jamal / Badi-ul-Jamal / **Badal Jamal** (1907) / **Badar Jamal** (2007) | Show the variants on screen |
| Devotion period | 12 years (1907, 2007) vs 40 days (modern tellings) | Say the versions differ |
| Ansoo Jheel | Absent from the 1907 and 2007 records; "said to" have been found by Air Force pilots in 1993 | Present it as a newer layer of the legend |
| Malika Parbat | 5,290 m vs "5,000 m" (UNESCO); a survey-map name locals didn't use (1907) | Don't link it to the fairy queen |
| When the lake got its name | Unknown; already named by 1883–84 | Say only "by 1883–84" |

## B. Questions for you (answers change the script)
1. **Lead version:** start with the **local Kaghan version** (Delhi/Mughal prince, demon, flood of Naran) and then reveal the 1,000-year journey? That's my recommendation. Or start with Mian Muhammad Bakhsh's Egyptian prince?
2. **Do you know anyone in Naran or Kaghan** (a hotel owner, jeep driver, guide, or someone's elder)? A **2-minute voice note** telling the legend "as our elders told it" would be the strongest unique asset. They could also tell us **where the "cave under a mound of graves" in Naran is** (UNESCO p. 36).
3. **Mian Muhammad Bakhsh verses:** do you want 1–2 Punjabi lines in the episode, read in your own voice? If so, which edition do you trust? I'll only use lines we confirm from a printed text.
4. **Earth Studio:** can you render the lake, the **valley from the lake down to Naran** (the flood path), Naran town and Malika Parbat? Coordinates are in `RESEARCH.md`.
5. **Tone of the ending:** keep it spooky ("woe to the mortal…") or end on conservation ("maybe the fairies left because we came")? I recommend both, in that order.
