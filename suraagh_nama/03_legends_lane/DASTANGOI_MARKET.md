# Dastangoi on YouTube: is it working? (checked 24 Nov 2026)

**Method:**
- 8 YouTube searches ("dastangoi", "داستان گوئی", "dastango", Mahmood Farooqui, Tilism-e-Hoshruba, Pakistan, and this-year filters) returned 2,472 videos. **836 of them are about dastangoi.**
- For the main channels I also pulled subscriber counts and the last 15 uploads (RSS view counts).

**Limits:**
- Search results are a sample, not all of YouTube.
- View counts are lifetime totals.
- Search results carry no upload dates, so recency comes from RSS.

## Conclusion
- **People search for the story, not for "dastangoi".** As a label or genre, dastangoi has a small, festival-driven, mostly Indian audience.
- **As a way of telling the story, it differentiates.** The Saiful Malook topic has proven demand (several videos over 100K views, one over 3M). None of the existing versions found is told by a dastango or checked against the 1907 record.
- **Himanshu Bajpai's channel shows the winning mix:** a popular subject, told in qissa style, short.
- **So:**
  - Keep the dastan style, but **don't put "dastangoi" in titles or thumbnails**. Sell the story and the mystery.
  - Put "داستان" in the description and tags only.
  - **Judge it by retention.** Compare the average viewing time with K2 and with the channel's 2:21 average, and watch the 0:30 drop-off. If the ornate opening loses viewers in the first 30 seconds, make the opening faster and keep the dastan voice for the legend.
