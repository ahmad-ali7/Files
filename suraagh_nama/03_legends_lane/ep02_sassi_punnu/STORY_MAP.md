# Ep 2 — Sassi-Punnu — Story map (hook-per-minute)

**Target:** ~7–8 min (Ep 1 standard). Dastan craft (حاضرین, character voices, verse, نشستیں), **not labelled**.
**Thesis:** the love story everyone knows hides a real dead city — Bhambore — and a mystery about which city it even is.
**Structure mirrors Ep 1:** cold real open → title → dastan → evidence → "the real force" → verse → teaser.

## Notes for script & build
- **Cold open must be real and eerie** (Bible §6): the ruins + wind + the vanished port, not "once upon a time."
- Keep **fact vs tradition** explicit on screen: "داستان کہتی ہے…" for the legend; dated/sourced facts for Bhambore.
- **The twist that makes it Suraagh Nama:** legend = memory of a real catastrophe (the city's death), + the Debal doubt.
- Reusable scene types already in the library: desert day-for-night, satellite of ruins, map route (Kech→Bhambore→
  Lasbela; Indus shift), evidence stills (ivory, excavation), painted dastan scenes, composited figures, verse-over-footage, dated timeline, sitting cards.
- **Character voices:** Narrator; the Raja; the washerman; Sassui; Punnu; the brothers (or a single brother); the shepherd; Verse (Bhittai).
- **Teaser for Ep 3:** pick from the backlog (e.g., Makli — "a city of the dead that is still full").
