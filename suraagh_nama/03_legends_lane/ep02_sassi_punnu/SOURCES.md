# Ep 2 — Sassi-Punnu — Sources & books to get

Ranked by value to the episode. **Access** marked: 🟢 free/public-domain · 🟡 snippet/library · 🔴 paywall.
The ⭐ items are the ones to get first.

## A. The on-screen "official document" (our 1907-Gazetteer equivalent) — SOLVED
- ⭐🟢 **Henry Cousens, *The Antiquities of Sind, with Historical Outline* (1929)** — Archaeological Survey of
  India, Imperial Series vol. 46. **Public domain, full scan + full text on archive.org.** The table of contents
  has a **chapter on Bhambor** — we can put a real 1929 page on screen, exactly like Ep 1's Gazetteer.
  - [archive.org copy 1 (full text)](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.36039) · [plain-text](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.36039/2015.36039.The-Antiquities-Of-Sind-With-Historical-Outline_djvu.txt) · [copy 2](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.200876)

## B. The excavation reports (the dig itself)
- ⭐🟡 **F. A. Khan, *Banbhore: A Preliminary Report on the Recent Archaeological Excavations at Banbhore*** —
  Dept. of Archaeology, Pakistan. Editions **1960 (26 pp) / 1963 (46 pp, illustrated, map) / 1976 (51 pp)**.
  Google Books snippet only; a **Univ. of Michigan scan** exists. This is *the* 1958–65 campaign source.
  - [Google Books (1963, snippet)](https://books.google.com/books/about/Banbhore.html?id=c2EGzgEACAAJ) · [WorldCat (find a library copy)](https://search.worldcat.org/title/banbhore-a-preliminary-report-on-the-recent-archaeological-excavations-at-banbhore/oclc/2205142)
- 🟡 **F. A. Khan, "Excavations at Banbhore," *Pakistan Archaeology* 1 (1964): 48–55** — the journal writeup.

## C. Modern Pak–Italian / French mission (recent data, PHOTOS, the river-death science) — several FREE

  - [PDF](https://easaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Felici-et-al-8.pdf)
- 🟢 **Sindh Antiquities 7 (2021), "Thorough Report of the Italian Team 2014–2015"** (IRIS, Univ. of Venice) — open PDF.
  - [PDF](https://iris.unive.it/retrieve/8f7991c3-4556-459e-80ac-414220b6edab/Sindh%20Antiquities%207%202021.pdf)
- 🟢 **Manassero & Piacentini Fiorani, Banbhore overview, *Silk Road* 12 (2014)** — open PDF.
  - [PDF](https://iris.unito.it/retrieve/e27ce42a-ef64-2581-e053-d805fe0acbaa/ManasseroFiorani_SR12_2014.pdf)
- 🟢 **Pakistan Archaeology No. 33 (2018)** — government journal, open PDF (context + other sites).
  - [PDF](https://doam.gov.pk/public/assets/assets/media/publications/20220519162901.pdf)
- 🟢 **Sindh Govt, "Technical Report on Excavation of Bhanbhore, Season 2018"** — names the French team's western trench.
  - [Sindh Culture Dept](https://antiquities.sindhculture.gov.pk/index.php/news/technical-report-on-excavation-of-bhanbhore-seasion-2018)
- 🔴 **Manassero/Piacentini et al., "Archaeological Excavations at Banbhore… 2014 & 2015 Field Seasons," *Parthica* 18 (2016)** — the core prelim report (paywalled; cite it).
- 🔴 **"Beyond the Fortified Town…," *South Asian Studies* 38:2 (2022)** — land use/occupation (paywalled; abstract free).
  - [Abstract](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02666030.2022.2111747)

## E. Bhittai / Sur Sassui (the VERSE spine — verify wording before screen)
- ⭐🔴/🟡 **H. T. Sorley, *Shah Abdul Latif of Bhit: His Poetry, Life and Times*** (Oxford Univ. Press, 1940) — the
  classic scholarly study + selections. Library/used; the standard citation for the verse.
- 🔴 **Hari Daryani 'Dilgir' & Anju Makhija, *Seeking the Beloved*** (Katha, 2011; Sahitya Akademi award) —
  modern English verse translation. Buy.
  - [Publisher page](https://readersend.com/product/seeking-the-beloved/)
- 🟡 **Amena Khamisani, *The Risalo of Shah Abdul Latif* (1994)** and **Elsa Kazi** (prose selections) — alt translations.
- 🟢 **Shah Jo Risalo / Bhittaipedia (online)** — for cross-checking Sur Sassui text.
  - [Shah Jo Risalo — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Jo_Risalo) · [Bhittaipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhittaipedia)
