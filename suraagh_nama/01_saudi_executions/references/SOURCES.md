# Sources: Saudi Arabia executions episode
Checked on 30 Oct 2026. Show each source on screen when its figure is spoken.

## Execution figures
- Human Rights Watch, 13 Jan 2026: https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/01/13/saudi-arabia-record-number-of-executions-in-2025
  - 2025: at least 356 executions (record; 345 in 2024). 240 for non-lethal drug offences, 188 of them foreigners (Reprieve and ESOHR count). 98 for hashish alone. Ended a 21-month unofficial moratorium on drug executions (late 2022).
- Amnesty International, Jun 2026: https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/06/saudi-arabia-almost-100-people-executed-this-year-for-drug-related-offences/
  - 1 Jan to 22 Jun 2026: 96 executions, at least 61 for drugs. 39 of the drug cases were foreigners: Ethiopia 7, Pakistan 7, Sudan 5, Jordan 4, Syria 3. Foreigners were 75% of drug executions in 2024 and 78% in 2025. Quote, Dana Ahmed (Amnesty): "a grim milestone exposing the authorities' unconscionable and unlawful use of the death penalty."
- ESOHR, first half of 2026: https://www.esohr.org/en/?p=15896 (108 executions, 71 for drugs; methodology differs from Amnesty)

## Cases
- Free Umrah with gift bags (ProPakistani, 9 Jan 2026): https://propakistani.pk/2026/01/09/police-bust-network-using-umrah-pilgrims-to-smuggle-drugs/
  - Dealer Shah Saddam lured people with free Umrah trips. Before leaving Islamabad, 5 pilgrims (3 women) received gift bags, clothes and shoes "to be collected at Jeddah Airport". About 5.5 kg of crystal meth was hidden inside. Each was sentenced to 25 years in Saudi Arabia. Kharrarianwala police (SHO Muhammad Riaz) arrested Saddam with 5+ kg of hashish.
- Mohammad Irfan (Dawn/AFP, Dec 2014): https://www.dawn.com/news/1148749/families-mourn-drug-mules-beheaded-in-saudi-arabia
  - 27, near Sargodha; his father is a tea seller. Two men promised a Gulf visa and tickets for $2,000; he sold his rickshaw and his wife's jewellery. In Karachi they threatened to kill him and forced heroin capsules into his body. Caught at Riyadh customs, sentenced to death after a brief trial. His father: the trade is "like a tree"; the Saudis cut the branches but "the trunk and the roots are still there". In 2014: 15 of 74 people executed were Pakistanis convicted of heroin smuggling.
- Sialkot airport, Aug 2025 (ProPakistani, 30 Aug 2025): https://propakistani.pk/2025/08/30/cocaine-seized-from-umrah-pilgrim/
  - An Umrah-bound passenger had swallowed 40 cocaine capsules (about 230 g). ANF arrested him and an accomplice in the parking area.

## Nazrul Islam (Makkah case): human details
- Prothom Alo English: https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/lawhyj6g64
  - 25, Brahmanbaria Sadar; cleaner at Masjid al-Haram; went to Saudi Arabia in Nov 2022 on a Tk 6 lakh loan (Tk 4.5 lakh still unpaid at arrest); arrested about 8 months after arriving; family learned a month later.
  - Last call 11 Oct 2026, about 2 to 2.5 minutes. On 14 Oct at about 7:30pm, someone phoned from Saudi Arabia to say he had been executed. His aunt Abeda Begum, 65, fainted and died that night.
  - Mother: "I want to see my son's body one last time." Father: "Now, I am just waiting to see my son's body."
- The Week, 20 Oct 2026: https://www.theweek.in/news/middle-east/2026/10/20/nazrul-islam-basir-execution-mecca-bangladesh.html
  - Sentenced under ta'zir. The consulate in Jeddah says it was never notified. Nazrul's father denies he was married to Siti Akhtar.
  - EXCLUDED from the script: the family's claim of a "dispute with a Pakistani co-worker". It is unverified and accuses an unnamed person.
