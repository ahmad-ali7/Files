# Market Size and Demand: Pakistan Fund Comparison / Analytics Product (as of Nov 2026)

Research cut-off: 2 Nov 2026. "Official" = MUFAP / SECP / SBP / PSX / Ministry of Finance / World Bank / AMFI documents. "Press" = newspaper or broker write-ups of official data. Conflicts are flagged inline.

## 1. Pakistan mutual fund industry: size, growth, mix, players

### Takeaway
Industry AUM is about Rs 4.7tr (Sep 2026, MUFAP via press). That is roughly 4x the Dec 2021 level and about 8x 2019, but growth slowed to +12% YoY in the year to Sep 2026, down from +38% and +68% in the two prior years. AUM is about 45–50% money-market and about 14% equity, and the Shariah share is about 44–48%. Assets sit with ~24 AMCs and 400–520 funds/plans, but the retail base is small: about 0.77m individual investors (Jun 2025).

### Cited Findings
**Total AUM and growth**
- End-Sep 2026 AUM was Rs 4.72tr, up from Rs 4.20tr in Sep 2025 (+Rs 520bn, +12.4% YoY). That is slower than the prior two years: Sep 2024 Rs 3.05tr (+Rs 1.15tr, ~38%) and Sep 2023 Rs 1.82tr (+Rs 1.23tr, ~68%). Source is MUFAP data reported by ProPakistani, 29 Oct 2026, which also says money market and equity funds saw negative growth in the latest period. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/29/mutual-fund-industry-adds-rs-520-billion-in-one-year/)
- Sep 2026 split by structure: open-end Rs 4.55tr; Voluntary Pension Schemes (VPS) Rs 155.49bn; employer pension funds Rs 7.41bn; ETFs only Rs 6.94bn. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/29/mutual-fund-industry-adds-rs-520-billion-in-one-year/)
- Sep 2026 split by holder: individuals Rs 2.04tr (43.2%); "others" Rs 2.19tr (46.3%); provident funds Rs 165bn; pension funds Rs 155bn; banks/FIs Rs 129bn; gratuity funds Rs 45bn. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/29/mutual-fund-industry-adds-rs-520-billion-in-one-year/)

**Unit-holder accounts**
- SECP, 2025: 768,769 individual mutual-fund investors and 6,361 corporate investors. Retail held 39.2% of AUM in 2025, up from 38% in 2019. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/08/17/pakistans-mutual-fund-industry-expands-nearly-sevenfold-in-six-years-reaches-rs3-93-trillion/)
- MUFAP unit-holder pattern, Dec 2025 (share of AUM): individuals 43%, others 51%, banks/FIs 3%, provident funds 3%. — [PACRA](https://www.pacra.com/view/storage/app/PACRA%20Research%20-%20Mutual%20Funds%20-%20March'26_1772786363.pdf)

### Inferences
- From Dec-21 (1,064) to Dec-25 (4,187), MUFAP AUM had a CAGR of ~41%. From Sep-23 to Sep-26 the CAGR is ~37%. Inflation, high policy rates and money-market inflows inflate the nominal figures. In USD terms growth is much lower, and the slowdown to +12.4% YoY (Sep 2026) is roughly flat in real terms. This is itself a case for a product that shows USD- and inflation-adjusted returns.
- The investable "fund-choice" market is narrower than headline AUM. About 45–50% sits in money-market funds and about 50% is held by institutions or corporates. Retail AUM is about Rs 2.0tr (Sep 2026), spread across ~0.77m individual accounts, which works out to roughly Rs 2.5m per individual on average. A small group of large holders probably skews that average.
- 400–520 funds/plans across ~24 AMCs is enough to make comparison hard for retail users: there are many near-identical money-market and income funds and a fast-growing "others" bucket (fixed-rate plans, FoF). That is the core use case for a screener.
- With only Rs 6.94bn in ETFs (Sep 2026) and one equity fund per category at most AMCs, a SPIVA-style "active vs index" scorecard would be new content locally.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 count of mutual-fund unit-holder accounts. The latest figure found is SECP's 768,769 individuals for 2025. MUFAP's monthly reports may contain it, but no fetchable copy was found.
- No official reconciliation of the SECP vs MUFAP AUM totals.
- No data on Total Expense Ratio (TER) distribution or front-end-load uptake across the industry.

## 2. Investor base: CDC/PSX accounts, overseas (RDA/NPC) demand

### Takeaway
Direct equity investors are few but growing fast. PSX had 583k UINs at 30 Jun 2026 (+48% in FY26) and 607k in Jul 2026, and ~85% of new accounts were Gen Z or millennials. Overseas Pakistanis have put $14.16bn into Roshan Digital Accounts, but mostly into Naya Pakistan Certificates ($2.1bn) rather than equities ($153m). That points to large demand for PKR yield and very little for active equity.

### Cited Findings
- Per the PSX Annual Report 2026, the investor base reached 583,052 UINs at 30 Jun 2026, against 392,775 on 1 Jul 2025. The 190,277 added was the largest single-year addition on record, and Gen Z and millennials made up ~85% of new accounts. — [PSX Annual Report 2026](https://dps.psx.com.pk/download/document/282570.pdf); [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/pakistan-stock-market-investor-accounts-jump-48-to-record-high-in-fy2025-26/); [Profit (SECP data)](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/09/pakistan-adds-over-190000-new-stock-market-investors-in-record-year-secp)
- UINs reached 607,025 in Jul 2026, the first time above 600k. Growth drivers cited: SECP removed duplicate onboarding documents for bank-onboarded investors and allowed trading accounts for minors. — [Pakistan Link](https://www.pakistanlink.org/Headlines/Aug26/07/04.htm)
- SECP's chairman has set a target of 2.5 million capital-market investors (press, via search summary). — [SECP / Profit coverage via search](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/09/pakistan-adds-over-190000-new-stock-market-investors-in-record-year-secp)
- In Jul–Mar FY26, mutual funds were net sellers of equities by Rs 47.7bn (Rs 889bn bought vs Rs 937bn sold, NCCPL LIPI data). — [Economic Survey FY26](https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey/chapter_26/5_Capital%20Market.pdf)
- RDA position at Sep 2026 (SBP data compiled by Arif Habib, via Profit):
  - Cumulative gross inflows $14.157bn (+$251m MoM); $2.146bn repatriated; $8.937bn utilised locally
  - Net repatriable liability $3.074bn
  - Islamic NPCs $1.375bn; conventional NPCs $740m; Roshan equity investments $153m (down $2m MoM)
  - — [Profit, 12 Oct 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/10/12/gross-inflows-into-roshan-digital-accounts-reach-dollar1416-billion-by-september-2026)
- RDA monthly inflows were $282m in Jul 2026 (+52% YoY) and $259m in Aug 2026 (+58% YoY). — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/10/roshan-digital-account-inflows-up-52-in-july-2026/)
- RDA account counts: more than 900,000 accounts by Feb 2026 — [Profit, 20 Feb 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/02/20/roshan-digital-account-surpasses-12-billion-in-inflows-with-over-900000-accounts-opened). 975,602 accounts by Sep 2026 — [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/roshan-digital-account-inflows-reach-14-16-billion/). The 975,602 figure comes from a search summary and was not re-verified on the page.
- **Minor conflict:** Profit reported $13.37bn cumulative inflows for Jun 2026, while ProPakistani and Alliance News put Jul at ~$13.65bn. Both are consistent with the monthly additions, and differences come from compilation. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/10/roshan-digital-account-gross-inflows-reach-dollar1337-billion-by-june-2026); [Alliance News](http://alliancenews.pk/others/roshan-digital-accounts-inflows-reach-13-65bn/)

### Inferences
- Roshan equity is only ~1.1% of gross RDA inflows ($153m of $14.16bn) and NPCs are ~15% ($2.1bn). Overseas Pakistanis clearly want PKR yield products and are wary of PKR equity, probably because of currency risk. A USD-adjusted return view answers their main question directly ("did I beat holding dollars?").
- RDA account holders (~0.98m) outnumber domestic PSX UINs (~0.6m) and individual MF investors (~0.77m). The overseas segment is a large, digitally onboarded, higher-income audience.
- The PSX investor base grew 48% in FY26 and is overwhelmingly young. That profile matches app-first, content-driven products.

### Gaps
- No 2026 CDC-specific investor-account or sub-account total was found. CDC's annual report was not retrieved. Note that UINs count accounts (NCCPL), not unique individuals.
- No overlap data between MF unit holders, PSX UINs and RDA holders, so the unique addressable population cannot be de-duplicated.
- No figure found for RDA balances invested in mutual funds specifically.

## 3. Digital adoption and digital onboarding

### Takeaway
Pakistan is mobile-first: ~161m broadband subscriptions (64% penetration), Raast with ~48m users, and 127m digital-banking users. Individual smartphone ownership is still only ~50% of people aged 10+. Digital investment onboarding exists (Sahulat accounts, digital AMCs, app brokers), but app user counts are mostly unverified company claims.

### Cited Findings
- Economic Survey FY2025-26: 161m broadband users, 64.2% broadband penetration, smartphone adoption 71.6%, mobile connection penetration 82.6%. — [PhoneWorld (Economic Survey)](https://www.phoneworld.com.pk/pakistan-smartphone-usage-71-percent-economic-survey-2026/); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/2007210)
- HIES 2024-25 puts household internet access at ~70% and smartphones in 96% of households. PTA's ICT survey says individual mobile/smartphone ownership among people aged 10+ rose from 45% (2019) to 50% (2025). — [INP WealthPK](https://www.inp.net.pk/article-detail/inp-wealthpk/internet-access-jumps-to-70-as-smartphone-use-reaches-96-nationwide); [CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/pakistan-sees-significant-growth-in-digital-connectivity-pta-highlights-hies-2024-25-findings/)
- Profit, May 2026, reports a usage gap: 81% mobile broadband coverage but a "52% internet usage gap", which it attributes to smartphone taxes. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/05/15/high-smartphone-taxes-leave-pakistan-with-52-internet-usage-gap-despite-81-network-coverage)
- Raast: ~48m users and ~Rs 50tr transacted in calendar 2025 (SBP Financial Stability Review 2025). Q2 FY26 (Oct–Dec 2025): 645.7m transactions worth Rs 18.5tr. Q1 FY26: 544m transactions worth Rs 12.8tr. — [INP / SBP FSR](https://inp.net.pk/article-detail/inp-wealthpk/raast-reaches-48-million-users-with-transactions-hitting-rs50-trillion-in-2025); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1983912); [INP Q1](https://www.inp.net.pk/news-detail/inp-wealthpk/raast-processes-544-million-transactions-in-q1-fy26)
- Digital banking users passed 127m (Mar 2026 headline). — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/03/03/raast-transactions-hit-rs1-6trn-as-digital-banking-users-cross-127m)
- NCCPL extended the Sahulat (simplified, low-risk) account facility to PMEX futures brokers through amendments to the CKO Regulations. SECP also operationalised a Capital Market Development Fund (CMDF) for financial literacy, and PSX moved to T+1 settlement on 9 Feb 2026. — [Economic Survey FY26, Box-2](https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey/chapter_26/5_Capital%20Market.pdf)
- In May 2026 SECP removed previous annual investment caps for small investors in mutual funds and simplified digital account opening (press headlines; full text returned 403). — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/05/14/small-investors-can-now-put-more-money-into-mutual-funds-under-new-secp-rules/amp/); [The Nation](https://www.nation.com.pk/15-May-2026/secp-makes-investment-mutual-funds-easier-small-investors)

- App brokers and platforms:
  - KTrade (KASB) markets "over one million users". This is a company claim and exceeds total PSX UINs (607k), so it likely counts downloads or registrations.
  - Finqalab's Google Play listing shows 50K+ downloads, and the app reportedly ranked 8th by active investor accounts ~15 months after its mid-2024 launch.
  - — [Investify / Digital Pakistan guides via search](https://www.investify.pk/blog/best-psx-apps-pakistan); [Google Play – Finqalab](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobile.finqalab&hl=en_US)
- Mahaana (YC W22, SECP-licensed digital AMC): first fund under SECP's Digital AMC framework in 2023; AUM Rs 4bn in Dec 2025 (0.1% share); launched a "100% digital retirement platform" in Feb 2026. — [PACRA](https://www.pacra.com/view/storage/app/PACRA%20Research%20-%20Mutual%20Funds%20-%20March'26_1772786363.pdf); [CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/mahaana-wealth-unveils-pakistans-first-100-digital-retirement-investment-platform/); [ProPakistani 2023](https://propakistani.pk/2023/04/27/first-fund-launched-under-digital-amc-framework-of-secp/amp/)

### Inferences
- Digital rails (Raast, digital banking) reach tens of millions, while investment accounts number in the hundreds of thousands. The gap between payments users and investors is roughly 50–100x. That is the funnel opportunity for an education-led comparison product.
- Lower front-end loads on digital channels (1.5% vs 3%) give users a concrete, quantifiable reason to compare channels and fees.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 user counts for KTrade, AKD Trade, Finqalab, Mahaana, Meezan's app or Sarmaaya's Zar. Business Recorder's Jan 2026 piece on digital investment platforms returned 403.
- No official share of new MF or PSX accounts opened digitally, and no Sahulat account count.

## 4. Financial literacy, inclusion and investor pain points

### Takeaway
Financial literacy is low: 26% of adults, against a 33% global average (SBP, 2023). Findex 2025 coverage cites only ~23% formal account ownership for Pakistan, though SBP's own inclusion figure is much higher. No sourced, Pakistan-specific evidence of fund mis-selling was found. The documented issues are fee and load complexity, and bank-driven flows into funds.

### Cited Findings
- The SBP 2023 survey found 26% of adults have adequate financial knowledge, against a 33% global average (cited by IBA research and SBP's Financial Literacy Week 2025). — [IBA iRepository](https://ir.iba.edu.pk/sbsic/2025/program/14/); [SBP PFLW 2025](https://www.sbp.org.pk/PFLW/index-14-18-Apr.html)
- SBP's National Financial Education Roadmap 2025-29 targets financial awareness for 50m people by 2029. — [The News](https://www.thenews.com.pk/print/1303146-sbp-aims-to-boost-financial-awareness-for-50m-by-2029)
- Global Findex 2025, as reported by The Financial Express (Bangladesh):
  - Only 23% of Pakistani adults have an account, but 90% of account holders made or received digital payments.
  - The gender gap is ~30pp in mobile-account ownership and >20pp in digital payments.
  - Pakistan is among the 8 economies holding over half of the world's unbanked.
  - **Conflict:** the same article elsewhere groups Pakistan with countries at "around 50%" account ownership. SBP separately says adult banking rose from 16% (2015) to 64% (2023).
  - — [Financial Express BD](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/bd-among-top-eight-countries-with-largest-unbanked-population); [World Bank Findex microdata (Pakistan)](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/7961/variable/F1/V30?name=fin4); SBP figure via [search summary of Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40356605/sbp-to-observe-financial-literacy-week)
- Fee and load complexity: loads go up to 3% (1.5% digital), and investor guides tell users to check the offering document rather than rely on a sales call. — [MCB FMR](https://www.mcbfunds.com/download/latest_fmrs_for_website/conventional_funds/MCB-Pakistan-Stock-Market-Fund.pdf); [Pakera guide](https://pakera.pk/mutual-funds-invest-pakistan/); [BlackIron Substack (opinion)](https://blackiron.substack.com/p/mutual-funds-vs-etfs-how-to-save)
- Complaints must go to the AMC first, then to the SECP portal (Jamapunji guidance). — [Jamapunji](https://jamapunji.pk/protect-yourself/complaint-registration-guidance-depending-nature-your-investment)
- Bank-driven distortion: in Dec 2024 banks steered large clients into money-market funds to avoid the ADR tax, and the money went back out after year-end (SECP official). — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/08/17/pakistans-mutual-fund-industry-expands-nearly-sevenfold-in-six-years-reaches-rs3-93-trillion/)
- SECP warned in May 2025 about fraudulent online trading platforms and investment schemes. — [SECP press release](https://secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Press-Release-SECP-Warns-Public-Against-Fraudulent-Online-Trading-Platforms-and-Investment-Schemes-1.pdf)
- PACRA's sector study already publishes risk-adjusted return analytics (downside deviation over 1, 3 and 5 years), but only for 66–81 PACRA-rated funds and in PDF form. — [PACRA](https://www.pacra.com/view/storage/app/PACRA%20Research%20-%20Mutual%20Funds%20-%20March'26_1772786363.pdf)

### Inferences
- Low literacy (26%) and heavy reliance on bank branch distribution (most large AMCs are bank-owned; see the market-share table) suggest a fund-choice process shaped by bank relationships rather than comparison. That is consistent with, but does not prove, the mis-selling hypothesis.
- Existing analytics (PACRA, MUFAP's daily NAV and return tables) are institutional and static. No source surfaced a consumer-grade comparison or scorecard product.

### Gaps
- **No sourced Pakistan-specific evidence was found of bancassurance or fund mis-selling reports, SECP complaint statistics by category, or surveys of how investors choose funds.** Searches returned only generic material and US precedents. This needs primary research or an SECP Annual Report deep-dive.
- No 2025/26 SECP investor survey or investment-awareness percentage was found.
- The Pakistan Findex 2025 account-ownership figure (23%) needs checking against the World Bank country table itself.

## 6. Comparable market: India (AMFI) as an analogue trajectory

### Takeaway
India shows what a "Value Research moment" looks like. Unique MF investors went from ~4.5cr (Mar 2024) to ~6.19cr (Jun 2026), folios from 8.27cr (Apr 2019) to 27.86cr (Jun 2026), and SIP accounts from 0.73cr (Mar 2015) to 10cr+ contributing (Aug 2026). Pakistan's ~0.77m MF investors is about 1/80th of India's unique-investor count.

### Cited Findings
- Folios: 8.27cr (Apr 2019) → 18.15cr (Apr 2024); 10cr crossed in May 2021; 22.08cr by 30 Nov 2024. — [PNB India page citing AMFI](https://dcmpx.remotevs.com/in/pnbindia/www/SL/mutual-fund.html); [AMFI](https://amfiindia.com/indian-mutual)
- June 2026: 27.86cr folios and 6.19cr unique investors (AMFI data via Nippon India and Angel One). The AMFI-Crisil Factbook 2026 has 6.1cr unique investors at Mar 2026. Unique investors were 4.5cr in Mar 2024, and the industry added ~1cr investors in FY25 (+22%). — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/mutual-funds/nippon-india-mutual-fund-crosses-4-crore-investor-folios-as-mutual-fund-industry-reaches-27-86-crore-folios); [AMFI Factbook 2026](https://www.amfiindia.com/uploads/AMFI_Factbook2026_14b34a6447.pdf); [Angel One FY25](https://www.angelone.in/news/mutual-funds/mutual-fund-industry-crosses-5-crore-investors-2nd-highest-growth-registered-in-fy25)
- SIP accounts: ~73 lakh (Mar 2015) → ~8.4cr (Mar 2024). In Aug 2026 there were 10.02cr contributing and 10.75cr outstanding SIP accounts, with Rs 32,297cr monthly SIP flow, and SIP AUM was ~21.4% of industry AUM. — [Shoonya](https://blog.shoonya.com/mutual-fund-growth/); [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/business/2026/10/07/contributing-sip-accounts-cross-10-cr-in-aug-2026-sip-assets-reach-21-4-of-industry-aum.html)
- AUM: Rs 10.9 lakh cr (Nov 2014) → Rs 68.08 lakh cr (Nov 2024), +524%. About Rs 82.6 lakh cr was reported for Sep 2026. **Conflict:** another report has ~Rs 87 lakh cr for Aug 2026, and folio counts for Aug–Sep 2026 also disagree (26.54cr vs 28.35cr). Verify against AMFI. — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/year-ender-mutual-funds-industry-aum-surges-over-500-pc-in-a-decade--20241231182411); [Fintechbiznews](https://fintechbiznews.com/finserv-mf-amcs/mf-industrys-aum-down-5)
- PwC projects Indian MF investors rising from 4.5cr to 26cr by 2047 (a forecast). — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/mutual-fund-investors-in-india-to-rise-from-4-5-crore-to-26-crore-by-2047-pwc-report/2531670)

### Inferences
- Penetration gap: India has ~6.2cr unique MF investors, about 4% of its population. Pakistan has ~0.77m individual MF investors, roughly 0.3% of a ~240m population. The population figure is the 2023 census figure, not re-verified here. Matching even half of India's penetration would mean ~5m Pakistani MF investors, about 6–7x today's base. This is an illustrative scenario, not a forecast.
- India's direct-plan and SIP boom coincided with comparison platforms (Value Research, Morningstar India) and with the 2012–2013 introduction of direct plans that removed embedded commissions. Pakistan's digital-channel load cap (1.5% vs 3%) is a partial analogue.

### Gaps
- No 2014 folio total or 2020 SIP-account count was found in this pass (AMFI monthly data would have them).
- Value Research and Morningstar India user or traffic numbers were not researched.
