# Regulation and Data Access: Pakistan Mutual Fund / ETF Comparison, Ratings and Analytics Product (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2 Nov 2026. About 20 search and fetch calls. Several primary SECP pages (the NBFC Regulations PDF and the Digital Distribution Platform guidelines page) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so some statutory detail is drawn from press coverage and is marked as such. **Nothing here is legal advice. Every licensing conclusion should be confirmed with Pakistani securities counsel (e.g., RIAA Barker Gillette, Haidermota & Co, Mohsin Tayebaly & Co) and/or SECP's licensing and innovation office.**

## 1. Licensing: what can be done without a licence, and what needs SECP authorisation

### Takeaway
Pakistan has no stand-alone "fund rating" licence for a third-party comparison site. The licensed rating framework (Credit Rating Companies Regulations, 2016) covers PACRA and VIS. Their fund "performance rankings" are star rankings of past performance, sold as part of their CRC business. MUFAP itself publishes monthly 365-day rankings of all CIS. The higher risks for a startup are (a) the Research Analyst Regulations, 2015 as amended in Jan 2026, which now *require SECP registration* for research analysts and reach social-media and digital publishers, and (b) the Investment Adviser licence under the NBFC Regulations, 2008 if the product gives personalised recommendations or robo-advice. Distribution (taking orders, sharing loads or commissions) requires being a licensed or approved distributor or platform under SECP's CIS distribution rules.

### Cited Findings
**Ratings / rankings framework**
- Credit rating companies are regulated under the Securities Act read with the Credit Rating Companies Regulations, 2016, promulgated 5 Aug 2016. Only two CRCs operate: PACRA and VIS Credit Rating Company Ltd. — [SECP: Credit Rating Companies](https://www.secp.gov.pk/licensing/capital-markets/credit-rating-agencies/)
- PACRA hosts a consolidated copy of the CRC Regulations 2016 "amended till September 19, 2022". — [PACRA-hosted CRC Regulations PDF](https://www.pacra.com/view/storage/app/announcements/reports/05-18-2025-01-40-30pmCredit-Rating-Companies-Regulations-2016-Amended-till-September-19-2022.pdf)
- The CRC Regulations require a CRC to "develop well defined rating criteria, methodologies and procedures for solicited and unsolicited credit ratings for each class of entity, instrument". — [same PDF, via search extract](https://www.pacra.com/view/storage/app/announcements/reports/05-18-2025-01-40-30pmCredit-Rating-Companies-Regulations-2016-Amended-till-September-19-2022.pdf)
- PACRA's fund performance ranking methodology:
  - The ranking is "an opinion on the fund's historical performance in comparison to other funds in similar category measured through a quantitative yardstick".
  - Fund categories follow SECP Circular No. 7 of 2009.
  - The score weights return and risk-adjusted return at 50% each, over 12, 36 and 60 month windows for 1, 3 and 5-year star rankings. Only funds operational for the full period are eligible.
  - Rankings are released twice a year (June and December). No Outlook or Rating Watch applies, and rankings are not buy, sell or hold recommendations.
  - — [PACRA Performance Ranking Report / Mutual Funds methodology (search extract)](https://www.pacra.com/summary_report/RR_517_14073_27-May-25.pdf)
- PACRA also publishes a separate Asset Manager Rating methodology. — [PACRA AMR Methodology](https://www.pacra.com/regulatory_disclosure/uploads/doc_report/AMR_Methodology_3_6_16.pdf)
- An SECP Direction (reported 21 Dec 2024) standardised CIS performance benchmarks:
  - **MUFAP** calculates and publishes category benchmarks and peer-group average returns monthly, monitors CIS performance disclosures, and **publishes monthly CIS rankings based on 365-day returns on its website**.
  - AMCs had to adopt the benchmarks and amend offering documents and FMRs within one month.
  - The benchmarks are built from PKISRV yields, which MUFAP announces daily.
  - — [Business Recorder, 21 Dec 2024](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40338855/collective-investment-schemes-secp-sets-standardised-performance-benchmarks); [Profit, 21 Dec 2024](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2024/12/21/secp-sets-performance-benchmarks-for-collective-investment-schemes)

**Research Analyst Regulations, 2015 (amended)**
- The original 2015 Regulations did not exempt anyone who must hold an Investment Adviser licence under the NBFC Regulations 2008. In other words, the two regimes sit side by side. — [Business Recorder, Mar 2015](https://fp.brecorder.com/2015/03/201503181162175/)
- SRO 748(I)/2017 amended the RA Regulations, including a definition of "white-labelling service provider" (a person who prepares research reports and sells them to a research entity for publication under its name). — [Business Recorder, Aug 2017](https://fp.brecorder.com/2017/08/20170804205149/)
- Draft amendments were issued in early Aug 2025, with comments due 15 Aug 2025. — [Profit, 4 Aug 2025](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/08/04/secp-issues-draft-amendments-to-research-analyst-regulations-2015); [Mettis Global](https://mettisglobal.news/SECP-opens-consultation-on-research-analyst-reforms-54251)
- Final amendments were notified (APP dateline "Thursday, January 8", consistent with 8 Jan 2026). The changes:
  - "now mandate SECP registration for research analysts"
  - broaden the scope of "research reports"
  - extend the blackout period
  - enhance target-price disclosures
  - clarify white-labelling
  - introduce a code of conduct
  - add compliance mechanisms aimed at individuals on social media doing analyst-like work
  - — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/business/secp-revamps-research-analyst-regulations-to-enhance-investor-confidence-transparency/); [Lead Pakistan](https://leadpakistan.com.pk/news/secp-revamps-research-analyst-regulations-to-enhance-investor-confidence-and-transparency/)

**Investment Adviser licence (NBFC Regulations, 2008)**
- The NBFC Regulations define "Investment Advisor" as an NBFC licensed by SECP to provide investment advisory services. Any NBFC licensed for a form of business under s.282A of the Companies Ordinance/Act must meet the minimum equity requirement "at all the times". — [SECP consolidated NBFC Regulations 2008, updated till 17 May 2023 (search extract; direct fetch blocked)](https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Non-Banking-Finance-Companies-and-Notified-Entities-Regulations-2008-Updated-till-May-17-2023.pdf)
- The May 2023 amendments let Investment Advisers offer portfolio management services to any investor, subject to a board-approved risk management policy and contingency plan. SECP described them as adapting to new technologies. — [SECP press release, 17 May 2023](https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Press-Release-May-17-SECP-amends-NBFC-Regulations-to-adapt-to-new-technologies.pdf); [ProPakistani, 17 May 2023](https://propakistani.pk/2023/05/17/secp-amends-nbfc-regulations-to-promote-financial-inclusion/)
- Business Recorder reports that SECP has authority to specify capital adequacy and minimum equity requirements for NBFCs. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40012335/nbfcs-capital-adequacy-minimum-equity-secp-enjoys-authority-to-specify-any-requirements)
- SECP issued an updated **Master Circular for AMCs and Investment Advisers** (reported 29 Jul 2026):
  - It consolidates all circulars and directions from 6 Jan 2009 to 30 Jun 2026.
  - Topics include Digital AMCs, digital onboarding via regulated financial institutions, performance benchmarks, Key Fact Statements, "digitisation, advertisements, and disclosure requirements", selling and marketing practices, sales load and expenses, mandatory certifications for NBFC professionals, distribution of CIS units, and outsourcing.
  - Where it conflicts with an individual circular, the individual circular prevails.
  - — [Profit, 29 Jul 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/29/secp-issues-updated-master-circular-for-mutual-fund-industry)
  - Earlier master circular editions: [Business Recorder 40133903](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40133903); [Business Recorder 40328311](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40328311/secp-issues-master-circular-for-amcs-ias); [SECP press release](https://www.secp.gov.pk/media-center/press-releases/secp-issues-master-circular-for-asset-management-companies/)

**Digital distribution / digital AMC / robo-advice**
- **SECP Circular No. 35 of 2020** introduced digital onboarding of mutual fund investors, with no physical presence or documents required. — [SECP press release](https://www.secp.gov.pk/media-center/press-releases/secp-allows-digital-accounts-opening-for-mutual-fund-investors/); [ProPakistani, 31 Dec 2020](https://propakistani.pk/2020/12/31/secp-allows-digital-accounts-opening-for-mutual-fund-investors/amp/)
- SECP notified **Guidelines for Mutual Fund Digital Distribution Platform** (June 2021). Press described such a platform as a one-stop shop letting investors, financial advisers and wealth managers choose retail investment products from a single point. The first SECP-licensed platform was due to go live mid-June 2021. — [SECP document page (fetch blocked)](https://www.secp.gov.pk/document/guidelines-for-mutual-fund-digital-distribution-platform/); [The Nation, 4 Jun 2021 (fetch failed, search extract)](https://www.nation.com.pk/04-Jun-2021/secp-notifies-guidelines-on-mutual-funds-digital-platform)
- In Apr 2022 SECP announced plans for a centralised digital fund distribution platform acting as intermediary between AMCs and investors. Implementation status was not confirmed. — [ProPakistani, 26 Apr 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/04/26/secp-to-unveil-centralized-digital-fund-distribution-platform-for-asset-management-companies/)
- SECP introduced the concept of **Digital AMCs**: all services online, from launching CIS to account opening, issuance and redemption, with testing planned in the sandbox. — [Business Recorder 40189521](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40189521)
- **Mahaana Wealth Limited** is the robo-advice comparable:
  - It describes itself as a licensed Asset Management Company **and** Investment Advisor, registered as an NBFC and regulated by SECP, and "Pakistan's first Digital Asset Management Company".
  - Client funds are held with CDC.
  - Its first fund launched under SECP's Digital AMC framework.
  - A $2.1m pre-seed round went toward regulatory capital.
  - Coverage cited an SECP statement on new licence categories, "digital Asset Management and digital Investment Advisors".
  - — [Mahaana company information](https://www.mahaana.com/company-information); [Business Recorder 40193078](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40193078); [Private Banker International](https://www.privatebankerinternational.com/news/mahaana-digital-wealth-management/)
  - The "first digital AMC" claim is self-reported. No SECP sandbox link for Mahaana was found.

**Regulatory Sandbox**
- SECP runs its Regulatory Sandbox in cohorts:
  - The first cohort allowed six solutions to test live. — [Business Recorder 40157257](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40157257)
  - The second cohort approved parametric insurance, real-estate tokenisation and centralised KYC solutions. — [SECP press release](https://www.secp.gov.pk/media-center/press-releases/secp-approves-various-technology-driven-solutions-under-2nd-cohort-of-regulatory-sandbox/)
  - A secondary source says 31 models were approved from 125 applicants across four cohorts in 2020–2023. Not verified on SECP's site.

**2025 NBFC amendments (fees / Shariah)**
- Final NBFC Regulation amendments (reported 15 Apr 2025):
  - Replace the TER cap regime with caps on management fees, effective 1 Jul 2025.
  - Require detailed TER disclosures.
  - CIS without a Shariah Compliance Certificate had to obtain one by 30 Sep 2025.
  - Funds structured like an existing Shariah-compliant scheme get relief from obtaining a separate certificate.
  - An Annual Shariah Advisor's Report must go in financial statements.
  - — [Mettis Global, 15 Apr 2025](https://mettisglobal.news/secp-notifies-amendments-to-nbfc-regulations-revises-ter-shariah-framework/); [ProPakistani, 16 Apr 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/04/16/secp-notifies-amendments-to-nbfc-regulations/)

### Inferences
- **Phase 1, likely low-licence-risk if carefully scoped:** a neutral, factual comparison or screener of publicly reported NAVs, returns, TER/fees, categories and benchmarks.
  - Use MUFAP/SECP categories and benchmarks.
  - Give no buy/sell/hold language or personalised recommendation.
  - Rank purely by stated quantitative criteria, the way MUFAP and PACRA do.
  - This is analogous to MUFAP's own 365-day rankings.
- **"Rating" wording:** calling a proprietary score a "rating" may invite confusion with the CRC regime. Neutral terms like "score", "screen" or "ranking by X metric" with a published methodology look safer. Counsel should confirm.
- **Research Analyst exposure:** after Jan 2026 the RA Regulations require registration and reach social-media and digital publishers. Any editorial "fund picks", target-based commentary or written research on funds/ETFs could fall within "research report". The broadened definition's exact text (whether it covers CIS units and quantitative screens) needs checking.
- **Investment Adviser licence:** personalised suitability, model portfolios tied to a user's risk profile, or robo-advice almost certainly need an IA licence (or digital IA category) under the NBFC Regulations, with minimum equity, fit & proper tests and Master Circular compliance. Mahaana's dual AMC+IA licence is the market precedent.
- **Distribution:** taking subscriptions, or receiving trail or load-sharing from AMCs, means operating as a CIS distributor or Digital Distribution Platform under the 2021 Guidelines and the Master Circular's "Distribution of CIS units" chapter. Referral or affiliate payments from AMCs without such status are a grey zone.
- **Sandbox:** this is a plausible route for novel models, e.g. aggregated digital distribution with AI-driven suitability.

### Gaps
- **IA minimum equity and fees:** the Rs amount, licence/application fees and processing time could not be verified. The NBFC Regulations PDF and SECP licensing pages blocked fetch. It is unclear whether a separate "digital investment adviser" category with lower capital exists in the regulations or only in policy statements.
- **RA Regulations amendment details:** SRO number and full text of the Jan 2026 amendment, the registration fee, eligibility and qualification requirements, and whether a fund-comparison website is a "research entity" were not found.
- **Distribution economics:** the 2021 Digital Distribution Platform Guidelines text was blocked (licence category, capital, investor-protection duties). I found no current sources on MUFAP individual-distributor registration, commission/trail-sharing rules or front-end load caps (Master Circular, sales-load section).
- **Sandbox currency:** no evidence of SECP sandbox cohorts after 2023, nor whether the sandbox is currently open.
- **Fund-ranking framework:** I did not find whether VIS's fund rankings, or PACRA's, are formally carried out under the CRC Regulations or as an unregulated ancillary product.

## 2. Advertising and marketing of fund performance

### Takeaway
SECP regulates AMC advertising and performance disclosure through the Master Circular (2026 consolidation), which has chapters on advertisements, disclosures, and selling and marketing practices. The Dec 2024 benchmark Direction standardised how performance is benchmarked in FMRs and offering documents. No source was found that sets rules for third-party sites showing fund performance, or for USD-converted returns.

### Cited Findings
- The Master Circular (Jul 2026) covers "Digitisation, advertisements, and disclosure requirements", "Selling and marketing practices for CIS", Key Fact Statement requirements and performance benchmarks. — [Profit, 29 Jul 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/29/secp-issues-updated-master-circular-for-mutual-fund-industry)
- Benchmark rules from the Dec 2024 SECP Direction:
  - AMCs must use the prescribed category benchmarks.
  - For 3 months they had to show old and new benchmarks in parallel in FMRs; after that, only prescribed benchmarks.
  - MUFAP "monitors CIS performance disclosures and ensures compliance".
  - — [Business Recorder, 21 Dec 2024](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40338855/collective-investment-schemes-secp-sets-standardised-performance-benchmarks)
- PACRA's rankings note that past performance is not a buy, sell or hold recommendation, and that rankings are comparable only within a category. — [PACRA (search extract)](https://www.pacra.com/summary_report/RR_517_14073_27-May-25.pdf)

### Inferences
- Even if third-party rules don't bind the product directly, it should mirror AMC norms:
  - compare funds only within the same SECP category
  - use SECP/MUFAP benchmarks
  - state return basis (annualised for money-market and income funds versus absolute for equity, as AMCs report)
  - add "past performance is not indicative of future results" and "investments are subject to market risk" style disclaimers
- Showing **USD-converted returns** is not regulated in any source found. It would be a derived calculation (NAV return combined with the SBP PKR/USD rate). It should be labelled as illustrative and not what PKR investors receive.
- If AMCs pay for placement or advertising on the platform, the AMC advertisement rules in the Master Circular may apply to that content, and the platform may need AMC sign-off.

### Gaps
- I did not find the exact past-performance presentation rules or mandatory disclaimer text (Master Circular advertisement chapter, MUFAP code of conduct / advertising guidelines). Verify against the full Master Circular PDF.
- No source addressed third-party (non-AMC) publication of fund performance or USD-return presentation.

## 3. Data availability and terms of use (NAVs, TERs, FMRs, PSX, SBP/PBS)

### Takeaway
Fund-level data (daily NAVs, monthly benchmarks and rankings, FMRs, offering documents) is published free online by MUFAP and AMCs. However, no explicit open-data licence was found, and MUFAP data is compiled from member AMCs. Commercial reuse terms are therefore unclear, and a data agreement with MUFAP and/or AMCs is advisable. PSX market data, including delayed and website/app display, explicitly needs a PSX licence; fees are not published. SBP and PBS macro data are public, but specific open-data licence terms were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- MUFAP's yearbook states that all information is "compiled from data provided by our member institutions". MUFAP offers a Daily NAV & Dividend email subscription. — [MUFAP yearbook notes](https://mufap.com.pk/pdf/yearbook/2018/Yearbook/Glossary&Notes.pdf); [mufap.com.pk](https://mufap.com.pk)
- Unofficial third-party scrapers already republish MUFAP NAVs and fund performance (Apify actors). They say they are not affiliated with MUFAP and that figures may lag official AMC notices. — [Apify MUFAP NAVs](https://apify.com/bakos_bence/mufap-mutual-fund-navs); [Apify MUFAP fund performance](https://apify.com/bakos_bence/mufap-fund-performance)
- MUFAP publishes monthly category benchmarks, peer-group averages and 365-day CIS rankings, plus daily PKISRV yields. — [Business Recorder, 21 Dec 2024](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40338855/collective-investment-schemes-secp-sets-standardised-performance-benchmarks)
- AMCs must publish monthly FMRs and offering documents and update them for benchmark changes. From FY2025-26, AMCs must make detailed TER disclosures. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40338855/collective-investment-schemes-secp-sets-standardised-performance-benchmarks); [Mettis Global](https://mettisglobal.news/secp-notifies-amendments-to-nbfc-regulations-revises-ter-shariah-framework/)
- **PSX data services page:**
  - Products: Real-Time Level 1; Level 1+ (10-level order book MBO/MBP); Level 2 (adds tick-by-tick); End-of-Day (OHLC, volume); Historical; and PUCARS corporate announcements.
  - Its legal notice says dissemination "in any form, live or delayed" via print, social media, websites or apps needs a PSX licence. Commercial use of website content, internal or as third-party derived data, needs prior PSX approval.
  - Approved vendors may redistribute only with PSX prior approval and licence to sub-clients.
  - No fee schedule is published. Contact: marketdatarequest@psx.com.pk.
  - — [PSX Data Services & Vending](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/product-and-services/data-services-vending)

### Inferences
- **Core comparison data (NAV, returns, TER, category, AUM, FMR holdings)** is all available, but should be sourced under explicit permission:
  - Approach MUFAP for a data-licence or API arrangement, or obtain AMC consent. AMCs have a commercial incentive to be listed.
  - Scraping MUFAP is technically feasible (others do it) but rests on unclear reuse rights.
- **ETF data:** ETF prices and iNAV-type intraday data come from PSX and need a PSX licence, even for delayed display. Phase 1 could show end-of-day NAVs (from MUFAP/AMCs) and avoid PSX prices, or budget for a PSX EOD or delayed licence.
- **Macro data:** SBP exchange rates and policy rate, and PBS CPI, are needed for real and USD returns. They are publicly published, but terms should be checked.

### Gaps
- I found no MUFAP terms of use or disclaimer text, and no official MUFAP or AMC API.
- PSX fee amounts for real-time, delayed, EOD and redistribution licences are unpublished. Request a quote from the PSX Market Data Team.
- I did not verify SBP data-portal terms (e.g., SBP EasyData) or PBS CPI reuse terms. They were not researched within the call budget.
- I did not check whether CDC (Central Depository Company) or NCCPL offer fund or investor data services relevant to aggregation.

## 4. Personal data protection, PECA, and Shariah labelling

### Takeaway
As of late 2026 Pakistan still has no comprehensive data protection statute. The Personal Data Protection Bill (2023 draft, cabinet-approved) has not been passed by Parliament. Personal data is governed by the Constitution, PECA 2016 (amended 2025), sector rules (SECP/SBP) and contract. For Shariah claims, the NBFC Regulations (2025 amendments) require Shariah Compliance Certificates and Shariah Advisor reports at the fund level.

### Cited Findings
- **Data protection status:**
  - The final draft PDP Bill 2023 was approved by Federal Cabinet and is pending approval of the National Assembly and Senate. — [Global Legal Post](https://www.globallegalpost.com/lawoverborders/data-protection-law-guide-1072382791/pakistan-1623790143); [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/pakistan-data-protection-cybersecurity/?export-pdf=)
  - The Chambers 2026 guide says the draft has completed consultation but has not been passed by both houses. — [Chambers Data Protection & Privacy 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan)
  - A business guide dated Oct 2026 says it has not been enacted. — [PAKCEC](https://pakcec.com/data-protection-law-pakistan/)
- Current protection comes from the Constitution, PECA 2016 and its 2025 amendment, sector laws and contracts. — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=PK); [Chambers 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan)
- Wikipedia describes a "National Commission for Personal Data Protection" as established. More recent legal guides contradict this, saying no active supervisory authority exists. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Commission_for_Personal_Data_Protection) contradicted by [Chambers 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan)
- **Shariah at fund level:** CIS without a Shariah Compliance Certificate had to obtain one by 30 Sep 2025. Funds similar to existing Shariah schemes get relief from a separate certificate. An Annual Shariah Advisor's Report must go in financial statements. — [Mettis Global, 15 Apr 2025](https://mettisglobal.news/secp-notifies-amendments-to-nbfc-regulations-revises-ter-shariah-framework/)
- SECP's benchmark framework defines separate Conventional and Shariah-compliant categories. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40338855/collective-investment-schemes-secp-sets-standardised-performance-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- A comparison site should only label a fund "Shariah-compliant" where the AMC's offering document and SECP category say so. It should cite the fund's Shariah Advisor and certificate rather than make its own Shariah determination.
- Any platform-level "Islamic" label for a model portfolio or robo product would likely need its own Shariah advisor or certification. This needs verification.
- Design the product to GDPR-like standards now (consent, purpose limitation, breach response). The PDP Bill could pass, and a later licensed IA or distributor role will bring SECP AML/KYC and outsourcing obligations (Master Circular, outsourcing chapter).
- PECA 2016 (amended 2025) brings cybercrime and content-liability exposure. This is relevant to user-generated content, forums and finfluencer-style content.

### Gaps
- I did not find whether a 2025 PDP Bill version exists separately from the 2023 draft. Data-localisation provisions in the draft were not researched.
- I did not verify the specific PECA 2025 amendment provisions affecting financial content platforms.
- I did not find whether SECP's Shariah Governance Regulations, 2023 (or similar) apply to non-AMC platforms using "Islamic" branding.

## 5. Business setup and tax basics

### Takeaway
The IT-export concessional regime appears relevant: 0.25% final tax on export proceeds for PSEB-registered firms, reportedly extended to 30 Jun 2029 in Budget 2026-27, per secondary sources. Most revenue from a domestic Pakistan fund-comparison product would be *domestic*, not export, so the benefit may be limited.

### Cited Findings
- The 0.25% reduced withholding / final tax regime on IT export proceeds for PSEB-registered companies was scheduled to expire 30 Jun 2026. P@SHA sought a 10-year extension. — [Business Recorder (BudgetFY26)](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40358496); [The News](https://thenews.com.pk/print/1302074-it-industry-seeks-10-year-tax-relief-for-export-growth); [P@SHA budget proposals](https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025-26-Budget-Recommendations-by-P@SHA-1.pdf)
- Secondary blogs report that the Federal Budget 2026-27 extended the 0.25% FTR (s.154A) to 30 Jun 2029. The conditions are PSEB registration and at least 80% of proceeds via formal banking channels; otherwise a 1% rate applies. — [cssprep.com.pk](https://cssprep.com.pk/freelancer-income-tax-pakistan/); [beancount.io guide, 13 Aug 2026](https://beancount.io/pt/blog/2026/08/13/pakistan-it-freelancers-0-25-percent-pseb-2029-guide). Not verified against the Finance Act 2026.
- Licensed NBFCs (IA/AMC) must be companies incorporated under the Companies Act with SECP and must meet minimum equity. — [SECP NBFC Regulations (search extract)](https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Non-Banking-Finance-Companies-and-Notified-Entities-Regulations-2008-Updated-till-May-17-2023.pdf)

### Inferences
- Incorporate as a private limited company with SECP from the outset. A later IA, distributor or digital AMC licence requires a corporate NBFC vehicle, possibly a public company for some licence types (to verify), with fit & proper directors.
- PSEB registration is cheap optionality if any revenue (data/API licensing to foreign investors, offshore SaaS) is export-based.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the Finance Act 2026 text, the PSEB registration fee or process, or the provincial sales tax on services (e.g., Sindh/Punjab IT services rates) for domestic subscription or advertising revenue.
- I did not research SECP company incorporation fees and timelines.
