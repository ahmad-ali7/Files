## Q1. Q3 2026 results (quarter ended 30 Sep 2026)

### Takeaway
Consolidated Q3 2026 revenue rose 29.5% YoY to Rs26.78bn, and PAT rose 8.7% to Rs3.03bn. The profit growth came from other income, lower levy and a negative Q3 2025 base (FX loss), not from operations. Consolidated gross margin fell from 30.1% to 24.8% and operating profit fell 7.5%. The standalone (unconsolidated) entity is weaker: Q3 PAT Rs1.533bn vs Rs1.998bn, down 23.3%. This confirms the PSX figure, which is the unconsolidated number. Management blames offshore wage and fuel inflation and an overvalued rupee (REER 109). About 80% of costs are people and over 90% of revenue is in foreign currency. Organic growth excluding Confiz is not disclosed.

## Q4. Why SYS fell about 35% in 2026

### Takeaway
I found no Pakistani press article that explicitly explains the 2026 de-rating. Primary evidence points to a combination of factors:
- Global AI-disruption fears for IT services and software. The company itself rebutted these in its HY deck, and peers sold off.
- Margin compression from an overvalued rupee and wage inflation, with falling standalone profits.
- Weak operating leverage: Q3 consolidated operating profit was down 7.5% despite revenue up 29.5%.
- About 3.9% dilution from shares issued to Confiz at Rs170.04, near the all-time high.
- New Middle East/Saudi risk.

The stock peaked at Rs174.40 on 31 Dec 2025, the day before Confiz shares were valued at Rs170.04.
